## Full Description (for Google Play Console)

Setlist Band Manager is the ultimate tool for musicians, bands, and worship teams to manage their shows, rehearsals, team members, and finances professionally, fast, and 100% offline!

🚀 Key Features:

• 🎵 Repertoire & Chord Management: Organize your songs with key, BPM/tempo, genre, lyrics, and full chords.
• 📋 Custom Setlists & Playlists: Build structured setlists for gigs or rehearsals, reorder tracks, and insert timed breaks.
• 🎤 Stage Mode with Auto-Scroll: View lyrics and chords in full screen with smart auto-scrolling and adjustable speed control.
• 📱 Quick QR Code Sharing: Transfer setlists and sync songs between devices or with the PC Web Editor instantly by scanning a QR Code!
• 👥 Band & Member Management: Keep track of band members, instruments/roles, and contact info with direct WhatsApp shortcuts.
• 💰 Gig Financial Tracker: Log earnings per event, track expenses, payouts, and monitor your band's financial growth.
• 📑 Professional Word (.doc) Export: Generate compact 2-column printable setlist sheets with a single click.
• 💾 Secure Backup & Data Export: Easily export and import your data when upgrading or switching phones.
• 🔒 100% Offline & Private: All your data stays safely stored locally on your device database.

Perfect for singers, guitarists, bassists, drummers, keyboardists, wedding bands, cover bands, and worship leaders.
